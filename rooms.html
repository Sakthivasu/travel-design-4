What time is check-in and check-out?

Standard check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Times may vary by property. Please confirm with your selected hotel.

Can I request a specific room?

You can mention your room preferences while making a booking request. Special requests are subject to availability.

Are children allowed in the rooms?

Family accommodation options are available at selected properties. Please check the occupancy details for your preferred room.

Are room prices inclusive of taxes?

Prices shown on this demonstration website are indicative starting rates. Applicable taxes and final pricing should be confirmed directly with the property.
